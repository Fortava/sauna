Why wear a sauna hat?

The hottest air
is above you.

Your head sits in the hottest part of the sauna and warms faster than the rest of your body. Wool felt holds a layer of insulating air around your scalp and ears, helping you stay comfortable and composed.

Temperature

Helps prevent your head overheating.

Dense wool felt acts as a thermal barrier, reducing direct exposure to the hottest air around your head and ears.

Comfort

Supports longer, steadier sessions.

Keeping your head more comfortable can make it easier to remain in the heat without feeling overwhelmed. Always listen to your body and leave if you feel unwell.

Hair

Shields hair from direct heat.

High temperatures can leave hair feeling dry and brittle. Wool felt covers the hair and helps reduce its direct exposure to intense sauna heat.

The material

The reason
is in the fibre.

Wool naturally regulates temperature by trapping air within its fibres. Felted into a dense, lightweight form, it becomes well suited to the heat and humidity of a sauna.

Why wear a hat in a sauna?+

Heat rises, so your head and ears often feel the intensity first. Wool creates an insulating air layer that softens that direct exposure while the rest of your body enjoys the heat.

Will one size fit me?+

The felt has natural give and the shapes are designed as flexible one-size styles. They sit over the ears without needing a precise fitted measurement.

How do I care for it?+

Air it after every session. If it needs a clean, use cool water and a gentle hand wash. Press out moisture without wringing, reshape and dry naturally away from direct heat.

Which shape should I choose?+

Dune is tall and playful, Coal is the timeless bell, and Ember has a softer rounded crown. Performance is the same—choose the silhouette that feels most like you.
