Material and function

Why
wool?

Your head sits in the hottest air in the sauna. Wool holds a quiet layer of insulation around your scalp, ears and hair.

Steadier heat

Helps soften direct exposure to the hottest air so the session feels more composed.

Protected hair

Covers hair from intense dry heat that can leave it feeling brittle and depleted.

Naturally suited

Breathable, renewable wool performs without synthetic padding or technical fuss.

Always listen to your body and leave the sauna if you feel unwell.
